BETTER JUDGMENT.
BETTER DECISIONS.

Kill Dull independently interrogates consequential decisions before organizations commit to them.

Not to make the decision. To improve the quality of judgment behind it — by exposing what the evidence supports, what the organization is assuming, and what the decision could strengthen or destroy.

01 · The problem

GOOD DECISIONS CAN STILL ADD UP WRONG.

It rarely starts with a terrible decision. Usually it starts with a reasonable one. Then another. Each makes sense on its own. Until the organization no longer quite resembles itself.

Kill Dull judges what a decision does to the company over time.

02 · Dense Ideas

WHAT MAKES GOOD DECISIONS ADD UP?

A Dense Idea is what an organization can keep making decisions from.

Not a tagline. Not a campaign. An idea strong enough to generate new decisions without losing itself. It tells an organization what it can do. And, crucially, what it shouldn’t.
03 · The criteria

WHAT DOES THE DECISION DO TO THE COMPANY?

The value isn’t in being smarter. Smart people already make these decisions. The problem is what gets considered — and what gets overlooked. Kill Dull codifies criteria for consequential decisions.

DistinctivenessMore recognizable or more interchangeable?
PreferenceStrengthen why people choose you or weaken it?
CoherenceCompound what matters or depreciate it?
ImitabilityCreate advantage or become easier to copy?
IdentityMake the company more itself or less itself?

Five criteria. Not the entire interrogation.
