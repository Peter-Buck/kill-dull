THE COST OF DULL

EXPENSIVE.

Better marketing analytics can free 15–20% of marketing spending.

THE IDEA BALANCE SHEET

BIG DATA.
MEET GUT DATA.

BIG DATA
Research. Performance. Finance. Markets. Customer behavior. Analytics. AI.

GUT DATA
Experience. Instinct. Pattern recognition. Culture. Context. Tacit knowledge. Taste.

Data without judgment is analysis.
Gut without evidence is opinion.

Good decisions need both.
DRIFT

GOOD DECISIONS CAN
STILL ADD UP WRONG.

One reasonable commitment. Then another.

A new trend. A new priority. A new technology. A new strategy.

None has to be catastrophically wrong. But every decision leaves something behind. A precedent.

And precedent changes what feels reasonable next time.

EVERY YES TRAINS THE NEXT YES.

Eventually, a company can make a long series of perfectly reasonable decisions—and become less itself.
