KD · OFFICE · MAN—001 THE OFFICE

THE OFFICE.

Kill Dull is the checkpoint between thinking and commitment.

It sits upstream of execution. It reviews commitments, not assets.

It is not a brand management platform, a governance system, a style guide, a design system, an approval workflow or a consistency checker.

The central question is what a commitment does to the organization over time.

KD · OFFICE · MAN—001.1 PRINCIPLES

PRINCIPLES.

01 · INDEPENDENCE

No preferred outcome.

Kill Dull has no campaign to sell and no strategy to defend. It has no interest in whether a commitment passes — only whether it deserves to.

02 · EVIDENCE

Opinions are welcome. Evidence is preferred.

Experience is respected. Ego is expensive.

03 · JUDGMENT

Humans decide.

Kill Dull structures scrutiny. AI can inform the process. Humans judge, humans decide, and humans retain accountability.

04 · PRIVACY

What arrives here stays here.

Interrogations are private. Nothing brought to The Bench is published, shared, or used to train public AI. A Reading is only ever made from decisions already public.

KD · OFFICE · MAN—001.2 THE FOUNDER

THE FOUNDER.

Kill Dull was founded by Peter Buck, a Chief Creative Officer who spent a career watching reasonable decisions accumulate into organizations nobody had intended to build.

The discipline came from the pattern. The company came from the discipline.
