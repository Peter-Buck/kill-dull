DISCIPLINE

HOW DO YOU JUDGE IT?

WHAT WILL THIS COMMITMENT DO TO THE COMPANY?

Every commitment is different.
The questions don’t change.

THE SEVEN STANDARDS
01DISTINCTIVENESSDoes this make the company easier to recognize — or easier to replace?
02PREFERENCEDoes this create another reason to choose the company?
03COHERENCEDoes this make the company add up better?
04IMITABILITYIf competitors copy this tomorrow, what will still be ours?
05IDENTITYDoes this make the company more itself — or less itself?
06PRECEDENTWhat will this yes teach the next yes?
07COMPOUNDINGWhat happens if this becomes normal?

SIMPLE QUESTION. SERIOUS SCRUTINY.

UNDER SCRUTINY

BEFORE WE JUDGE ANYTHING, WE ESTABLISH WHAT IS TRUE.

A commitment doesn’t exist in isolation.

It enters a market. A company. A customer relationship. A balance sheet. A history of previous decisions.

So judgment starts with reality.

DIAGNOSIS
MARKET ORIENTATIONWhat is true outside the building?
MARKET POSITIONWhere does the company actually stand?
BRAND REALITYWhat, if anything, exists in people’s minds about the company?
COMMERCIAL REALITYWhat resources and constraints sit behind the ambition?
DENSE IDEA STATUSWhat has the company already accumulated that is worth protecting or compounding?
MARKETING JOBWhat actually needs to happen across Product, Price, Place and Promotion?

We don’t need to know everything.

We need to know enough to judge responsibly.

ENOUGH TO DECIDE. NOT ENOUGH TO KNOW EVERYTHING.

EVIDENCE

WHAT DO WE ACTUALLY KNOW?

Every material claim earns a status.

KNOWNEstablished.
INDICATEDSupported, but not established.
UNKNOWNMaterial information is absent or insufficient.

UNKNOWN IS A FINDING.

Evidence comes in more than one form.

BIG DATA

Research. Behavior. Performance. Economics. Markets. Measurement.

GUT DATA

Experience. Instinct. Pattern recognition. Culture. Tacit knowledge.

Neither automatically outranks the other.

When they agree, that matters.

When they disagree, that matters more.

We don’t average contradictions away.

TENSION STAYS TENSION UNTIL IT EARNS RESOLUTION.

JUDGMENT

NOW PUT THE COMMITMENT UNDER PRESSURE.

The Seven Standards are not a scorecard.

They don’t carry fixed weights. They aren’t averaged into a reassuring number with two decimal places.

A pricing decision can put different things at risk than a market entry. A partnership can create different consequences than a product launch. Context determines what is material.

The question remains:

WHAT WILL THIS COMMITMENT DO TO THE COMPANY?

Not only tomorrow.

Over time.

THE COUNTERFACTUAL

Judging the commitment isn’t enough.

We also ask:

IF WE DON’T DO THIS, WHAT HAPPENS INSTEAD?

Because doing nothing is still a decision.

And sometimes the safest-looking option carries the larger risk.

THE DENSE IDEA BALANCE SHEET

Every consequential yes gives something and takes something.

So we make the trade visible.

The balance sheet doesn’t calculate the answer.

It makes the cost of the answer harder to ignore.

JUDGMENT IS NOT ARITHMETIC.

RECOMMENDATION

EVENTUALLY, SOMEONE HAS TO TAKE A POSITION.

Evidence informs the judgment.

AI interrogates the case.

A human takes responsibility for the recommendation.

AAH.The commitment deserves to be made.
HMM.The evidence or judgment is insufficient to responsibly recommend for or against the commitment in its present state.
DULL.The commitment does not deserve to be made in its present form.

AI DOESN’T GET THE LAST WORD.

NEITHER DOES CONSENSUS.

Because judgment without accountability is just an opinion.
