01 / DIAGNOSIS

ESTABLISH WHAT IS TRUE.

Diagnosis establishes the conditions in which the proposed commitment must work. It precedes evaluation of the commitment itself.

01 / MARKET ORIENTATIONWhat is true outside the building?
02 / MARKET POSITIONWhere does the company actually stand?
03 / BRAND REALITYWhat, if anything, exists in people's minds about the company?
04 / COMMERCIAL REALITYWhat resources and constraints sit behind the ambition?
05 / DENSE IDEA STATUSWhat has the company accumulated?
06 / MARKETING JOBWhat actually needs to happen across Product, Price, Place and Promotion?

EVIDENCE STATUS

KNOWNSupported sufficiently to be treated as established.
INDICATEDSupported, but not sufficiently to be treated as established.
UNKNOWNMaterial information is absent or insufficient.

Unknown is a finding.

Diagnosis does not require complete knowledge. It requires sufficient reality to proceed responsibly.

ENOUGH TO DECIDE. NOT ENOUGH TO KNOW EVERYTHING.

02 / JUDGMENT

EXPOSE THE TOTAL TRADE.

Judgment examines what the proposed commitment will do to the company.

The commitment is considered in relation to its immediate effects, commercial consequences, existing assets, future implications and counterfactual.

WHAT WILL THIS COMMITMENT DO TO THE COMPANY?

THE SEVEN STANDARDS

01DISTINCTIVENESSDoes this make the company easier to recognize — or easier to replace?
02PREFERENCEDoes this create another reason to choose the company?
03COHERENCEDoes this make the company add up better?
04IMITABILITYIf competitors copy this tomorrow, what will still be ours?
05IDENTITYDoes this make the company more itself — or less itself?
06PRECEDENTWhat will this yes teach the next yes?
07COMPOUNDINGWhat happens if this becomes normal?

The standards are not converted into a composite score. They do not carry fixed weights. Their materiality depends on the commitment being judged.

JUDGMENT IS NOT ARITHMETIC.

EVIDENCE

BIG DATA

Quantitative and externally observable evidence.

Research. Behavior. Performance. Economics. Markets. Measurement.

GUT DATA

Human and experiential evidence.

Experience. Instinct. Pattern recognition. Culture. Tacit knowledge.

Neither form automatically outranks the other. Neither may be represented as the other. Material contradictions are retained until they can be responsibly resolved or recorded as tension or uncertainty.

THE COUNTERFACTUAL

IF WE DO NOT MAKE THIS COMMITMENT, WHAT HAPPENS INSTEAD?

Doing nothing is not treated as consequence-free. The proposed commitment is judged against its meaningful alternative, not against an imaginary condition in which no decision has consequences.

THE DENSE IDEA BALANCE SHEET

The Dense Idea Balance Sheet records what the proposed commitment adds and what it takes away.

STRENGTHENINGWEAKENING

The Balance Sheet does not calculate the recommendation. Its purpose is to make the exchange visible.

03 / RECOMMENDATION

TAKE A POSITION.

Recommendation follows completed Diagnosis and Judgment.

DOES THIS COMMITMENT DESERVE TO BE MADE?

AAH.PROCEEDThe commitment deserves to be made.
HMM.UNRESOLVEDThe Discipline cannot responsibly recommend for or against the commitment in its present state.
DULL.DO NOT PROCEEDThe commitment does not deserve to be made in its present form.

The recommendation is not a prediction of success. It is a position on whether the commitment deserves to be made given the available evidence, consequences, uncertainties and alternatives.

AI RECOMMENDATION

AI produces an independent recommendation from the completed Judgment Record. The AI must take a position. Its recommendation is locked before human review.

HUMAN JUDGMENT

A human reviews the completed record and the locked AI recommendation. The human judges the recommendation rather than repeating the analysis.

Agreement is recorded. Disagreement is recorded. Material disagreement requires reasoning.

Human judgment determines Kill Dull's final recommendation because institutional judgment requires accountability. Not because humans are assumed to be infallible.

CLIENT DECISION

Kill Dull makes a recommendation. The client retains the decision.

THE DISCIPLINE RECOMMENDS.
THE CLIENT DECIDES.

04 / THE READING

PUT IT ON RECORD.

The Reading is the finished expression of the Discipline. It is not another analytical stage. It does not reopen the Judgment.

It records the judgment that was made and the basis on which it was made.

THE RECORD

  • READING ID
  • DATE

  • READING VERSION

The recommendation appears first. The machinery does not.

CONSIDERED. CONCLUDED. ON RECORD.

PRIVATE READING

A Private Reading is conducted before commitment. It is commissioned by the client. It is confidential. The client receives the completed Reading as a durable digital record and physical book. A Private Reading remains private.

PUBLISHED READING

A Published Reading examines a commitment after it has become publicly observable. It is initiated by Kill Dull. It uses public evidence. It applies the same Discipline. It is published as an independent judgment.

A Published Reading is not an advertisement rating, review, award, takedown or retrospective consulting exercise.

SAME DISCIPLINE. DIFFERENT MOMENT.

INDEPENDENCE

Completion of a Reading ends the engagement unless a separate new Reading is commissioned.

  • Develop the strategy.
  • Rewrite the proposition.
  • Develop the campaign.
  • Redesign the product.
  • Implement the recommendation.
  • Police execution.
  • Manage the brand.

A DULL recommendation does not create a downstream consulting assignment.

NOTHING FOR SALE BUT THE JUDGMENT.

THE RECORD OVER TIME

One Reading records one judgment. Across many Readings, the record becomes evidence.

It can show where evidence and experience agreed; where they diverged; where AI recommendation and human judgment differed; which assumptions proved material; which consequences emerged; which commitments compounded; and which depreciated.

The purpose is not to establish a record of being right. It is to improve the quality of future judgment.
