01 / DIAGNOSIS

ESTABLISH WHAT IS TRUE.

Diagnosis establishes the conditions in which the proposed commitment must work. It precedes evaluation of the commitment itself.

01 / MARKET ORIENTATIONWhat is true outside the building?
02 / MARKET POSITIONWhere does the company actually stand?
03 / BRAND REALITYWhat, if anything, exists in people's minds about the company?
04 / COMMERCIAL REALITYWhat resources and constraints sit behind the ambition?
05 / DENSE IDEA STATUSWhat has the company accumulated?

02 / JUDGMENT

EXPOSE THE TOTAL TRADE.

Judgment examines what the proposed commitment will do to the company.

WHAT WILL THIS COMMITMENT DO TO THE COMPANY?

THE SEVEN STANDARDS

01DISTINCTIVENESSDoes this make the company easier to recognize — or easier to replace?
02PREFERENCEDoes this create another reason to choose the company?
03COHERENCEDoes this make the company add up better?
04IMITABILITYIf competitors copy this tomorrow, what will still be ours?
05IDENTITYDoes this make the company more itself — or less itself?
06PRECEDENTWhat will this yes teach the next yes?
07COMPOUNDINGWhat happens if this becomes normal?

No composite score. No fixed weights. Materiality depends on the commitment.

JUDGMENT IS NOT ARITHMETIC.

EVIDENCE

BIG DATA

Research. Behavior. Performance. Economics. Markets. Measurement.

GUT DATA

Experience. Instinct. Pattern recognition. Culture. Tacit knowledge.

Neither automatically outranks the other. Contradictions are retained as tension or uncertainty.

THE COUNTERFACTUAL

IF WE DO NOT MAKE THIS COMMITMENT, WHAT HAPPENS INSTEAD?

Doing nothing is not consequence-free.

03 / RECOMMENDATION

TAKE A POSITION.

DOES THIS COMMITMENT DESERVE TO BE MADE?

AAH.RECOMMENDThe commitment deserves to be made.
HMM.UNRESOLVEDCannot responsibly recommend for or against in its present state.
DULL.DO NOT RECOMMENDThe commitment does not deserve to be made in its present form.

AI produces an independent recommendation from the completed Judgment Record. That recommendation is locked before human review.

A human reviews the completed record and the locked AI recommendation. Agreement or disagreement is recorded. Material disagreement requires reasoning.

The human determines Kill Dull's final recommendation because institutional judgment requires accountability.

THE DISCIPLINE RECOMMENDS. THE CLIENT DECIDES.

04 / THE READING

PUT IT ON RECORD.

The Reading is the finished expression of the judgment. It is not another analytical stage and does not reopen Judgment.

It records the recommendation and its basis, including the commitment, evidence cutoff, Discipline version, AI recommendation, human responsibility, material tensions and what would change the recommendation.

CONSIDERED. CONCLUDED. ON RECORD.

PRIVATE READING

Commissioned before commitment. Confidential. A durable digital record and physical book.

PUBLISHED READING

Initiated by Kill Dull after a commitment is publicly observable. Public evidence. Same Discipline. Independent judgment.

SAME DISCIPLINE. DIFFERENT MOMENT.

INDEPENDENCE

Kill Dull does not automatically develop strategy, rewrite propositions, develop campaigns, redesign products, implement recommendations, police execution or manage brands.

NOTHING FOR SALE BUT THE JUDGMENT.

THE RECORD

One Reading records one judgment. Across many, the record becomes evidence: where evidence and experience agreed or diverged, where AI and human judgment differed, which assumptions mattered and which commitments compounded or depreciated.
