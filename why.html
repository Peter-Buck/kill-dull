WHYWHATHOWOFFEROUTCOME
WHAT WE BELIEVE

Some ideas keep on giving.

Not because they say more. Because there’s more in them. They solve something people care about. They give a company somewhere to go. Then somewhere else. And somewhere else after that. New products. New experiences. New reasons to choose. New ways to grow. The more you build on them, the more they give back.

We call them Dense Ideas.

AN EXAMPLE OF A DENSE IDEA

A brick.

Not much to look at.A lump of earth, really.Made square.

And yet.

We’ve made houses from them.Places to be born in.Fall in love in.Hide in.Pray in.Make things in.Lock people in.

We’ve built walls to keep people out.And cities to bring people together.

We’ve fought over those cities.Burned them.Bombed them.Built them again.

We built cathedrals to get closer to God.And palaces for people who thought they’d live forever.

They didn’t.The bricks did.

The strange thing is, the brick has never had an opinion.It doesn’t tell you what to build.It just gives you something to build with.

A home.A hospital.A prison.A pub.Your call.

Funny how something so simple just keeps on giving.
