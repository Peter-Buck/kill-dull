HOW THE BENCH WORKS

THREE PARTS. ONE DECISION.

01 · THE DECISION

BIG ENOUGH TO MATTER.
EARLY ENOUGH TO STOP.

A product launch. A market entry. A repositioning. A brand architecture decision. A name. A partnership. A customer-experience change. A major AI commitment. A distribution change.

The subject can change. The condition doesn’t.

Not an idea you might explore.
A commitment you are actually considering.

02 · THE BENCH

YOU COME IN.
THEN YOU LEAVE.

You bring the proposed commitment, why you believe in it and the evidence that matters.

Then you leave.

No workshop. No steering committee. No weekly PowerPoint tennis.

Independent scrutiny begins. If something material is missing, we ask.

We don’t execute the decision. We don’t produce the work. We don’t profit from what comes next.

A no is as successful for us as a yes. We are outside the outcome.
