GO DEEPER

Big Data + Gut Data

Evidence, research, performance, finance, markets and AI meet experience, instinct, pattern recognition, culture and tacit knowledge. Neither gets to masquerade as the other.

The questions

What does the evidence support? What are we assuming? What could this strengthen? What could this destroy?

The standards

Distinctiveness. Preference. Coherence. Imitability. Identity. Precedent. Compounding.

Every yes trains the next yes

BENCH looks beyond the immediate result to what approving this commitment teaches the organization to approve next.

AI’s role

AI can research, compare, organize evidence, surface contradictions, challenge assumptions and run scenarios. AI assists the scrutiny. Judgment stays human. Decision stays with the client.
