BIG DATA

Research. Behavior. Performance. Economics. Markets. Measurement.

GUT DATA

Experience. Instinct. Pattern recognition. Culture. Tacit knowledge.

Neither automatically outranks the other. When they agree, that matters. When they disagree, that matters more.

JUDGMENT

NOW PUT THE COMMITMENT UNDER PRESSURE.

A pricing decision puts different things at risk than a market entry. A partnership creates different consequences than a product launch. Context determines what matters.

The question remains:

What will this commitment do to the company?

Not only tomorrow. Over time.

The Counterfactual

Judging the commitment isn’t enough. We also ask:

If we don’t do this, what happens instead?

Doing nothing is still a decision. Sometimes the safest-looking option carries the larger risk.
