HOW DO YOU JUDGE A MARKETING COMMITMENT?

What will this commitment do for the customer — and to the company?

Every commitment is different. The questions don’t change.

01RELEVANCEDoes this solve a real customer problem or meet an expectation that matters?
02DISTINCTIVENESSDoes this make the company easier to recognize — or easier to replace?
03PREFERENCEDoes this create another reason to choose the company?
04COHERENCEDoes this make the company add up better?
05IMITABILITYIf competitors copy this tomorrow, what will still be ours?
06IDENTITYDoes this make the company more itself — or less itself?
07PRECEDENTWhat will this yes teach the next yes?
08COMPOUNDINGWhat happens if this becomes normal?
HUMANWhat might people do that the evidence says they shouldn’t?
CULTUREWhat does this decision mean in the current and future culture it’s entering?
ECONOMICSWhat changes when customers feel richer — or poorer?
POLITICSWhat could politics or policy change about this decision?
