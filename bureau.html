01 · WHY WE EXIST

MARKETING MAKES BIG DECISIONS.

Product. Price. Place. Promotion.

Decisions that can build distinctiveness, preference and pricing power. Or quietly take them away.

Companies have specialists for every part of marketing. Research. Strategy. Agencies. Analytics. Effectiveness. Finance.

What they don’t always have is an independent place to put the whole decision under scrutiny before they commit.

That’s why Kill Dull exists.

THE DENSE IDEA COMPANY.

Kill Dull brings independent scrutiny to consequential marketing decisions before commitment.

At its heart is the Bench. One decision comes in. Evidence and assumptions are interrogated. The consequences across Product, Price, Place and Promotion are considered together. A verdict comes back.

What the company does with it remains the company’s decision. That’s the point.

03 · THE RECORD

US, UNDER SCRUTINY.

We ask other companies to withstand scrutiny. Seems reasonable that we should too.

Founder

Peter Buck.

Kill Dull was founded by Peter Buck, who spent a career on both the agency and client side of consequential marketing decisions. And kept noticing the same thing.

Companies could spend extraordinary amounts of money researching, making, distributing and measuring a decision. Independent scrutiny before making it was harder to find.

Kill Dull came from that gap.

Independence
