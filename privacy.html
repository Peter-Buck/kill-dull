Privacy.

What arrives here stays here.

Kill Dull respects the privacy of the people who visit the site, contact us and bring decisions to the Bench.

Information we receive

We receive information when you contact Kill Dull or work with us directly.

This site has no accounts and no sign-up. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics unless you accept them. The contact form sends us only what you choose to enter — your name, company, email address and message — by email; the site does not store it. Standard request information, such as IP address and browser type, is processed by our hosting provider in the course of serving the site.

Cookies and analytics

Analytics are off unless you accept them. Decline and nothing is collected: no cookie is set, and no record of your visit is made.

If you accept, one first-party cookie lets us tell one visit from another. We record which pages are read, roughly how long for and how far down, where the visit arrived from, and the deliberate actions the site offers — sending the contact form, opening a Reading, following a link out. We do not record what you type. What you write in the contact form is never analytics data.

As the page is served, your IP address is used once to establish the network the visit came from and the approximate city. It is used for that lookup and discarded. It is not stored, not written to any log of ours, and never sent to the analytics service.

That can tell us an organization, or that a visit came from a home connection, a mobile network or a VPN. It does not tell us who you are. We do not identify individual people and we do not try to work one out from a company, a network or a location. Where the organization is genuinely identified, the record still says the individual is unknown; most visits are simply recorded as anonymous.

You can change your mind at any time using Cookie settings at the foot of any page.

What we use it for

We use information to respond to inquiries, provide requested services and maintain security.

We don’t sell personal information.

The Bench

Information brought to the Bench is treated as confidential. Material provided for a private engagement is not published without permission. We do not use confidential client material to train public AI models.

Where third-party services assist that work, information is shared only as necessary to provide the service.

Service providers

The public site is served by Vercel, which hosts it and delivers it. Google carries the contact form to us through our own Workspace mailbox, and so receives what you wrote in it.

If you accept analytics, two further services are involved. PostHog stores the events, on servers in the European Union. Ipregistry resolves the request’s network to an organization and an approximate city, and receives the IP address for that lookup and nothing else — no cookie, no identifier, no page.

If you decline, neither receives anything about your visit. We do not authorize providers to use information for their own unrelated purposes.

Links

The site may link to websites operated by other organizations. Their privacy practices are their responsibility.

Your information

If you want to ask what personal information we hold about you, request a correction or deletion, or raise a privacy concern, contact us. Applicable privacy rights may vary depending on where you live.

To withdraw analytics consent, use Cookie settings at the foot of any page.

Changes

We may update this policy as Kill Dull and the services we use change. The current version will always be published here.
